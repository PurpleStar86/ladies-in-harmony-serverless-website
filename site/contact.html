Founding Story

(In 2019, this prayer movement began as a simple act of obedience a 100-day walk with God. Two members of our prayer group committed to writing daily thank-you notes to God and praying with at least one person each day. What started as a 100-day commitment became a lifestyle of consistent prayer, gratitude, and community. When the 100 days ended, we felt led not to stop, but to continue. In 2024, another prayer partner joined us and shared a prophetic word that God desired for this prayer line to be extended and made public. We did not move hastily. Instead, we prayed, fasted, and sought God’s direction for confirmation and clarity, including the name and structure of the prayer line. On March 16, 2024, we officially launched publicly on Zoom. Since that day, the prayer line has continued faithfully and consistently, meeting three times a day—7:30 AM, 1:00 PM, and 7:30 PM. What began privately has grown into a shared space for prayer, encouragement, and spiritual covering. Our mission remains simple: to create a consistent place where people can come together to seek God, give thanks, and pray in unity. We believe in the power of prayer, the importance of obedience, and the fruit that comes from consistency. This prayer line exists to serve, uplift, and glorify God one prayer at a time
